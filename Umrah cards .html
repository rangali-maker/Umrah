UMRAH COMPANION

Your Umrah,
one step at a time.

Only the essential duas. Clear, calm and easy to use.

YOUR JOURNEY
01 · INTENTION & IHRAM
Begin your Umrah
Intention
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ عُمْرَةً
Labbayka Allahumma ‘Umrah.
Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah.
The intention is in the heart.
Talbiyah
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbayka Allahumma labbayk, labbayka la sharika laka labbayk, innal-hamda wan-ni‘mata laka wal-mulk, la sharika lak.
Here I am, O Allah, here I am. You have no partner. Surely all praise, blessing and dominion belong to You.
02 · MASJID AL-HARAM
Entering & leaving
Entering
اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Allahumma iftah li abwaba rahmatik.
O Allah, open for me the gates of Your mercy.
Leaving
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
Allahumma inni as’aluka min fadlik.
O Allah, I ask You from Your bounty.
03 · TAWAF
The essential duas
At the Black Stone
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allahu akbar.
Allah is the Greatest.
Between Yemeni Corner & Black Stone
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina ‘adhaban-nar.
Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
For the rest of Tawaf: there is no authentic specific dua for each circuit. Make sincere personal dua, remember Allah, or recite Qur'an.
04 · SAFA & MARWA
Sa'i
When first approaching Safa
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ
Innas-Safa wal-Marwata min sha'a'irillah.
Indeed, Safa and Marwa are among the symbols of Allah.
Then say: Abda’u bima bada’allahu bih — “I begin with what Allah began with.”
At Safa & Marwa
لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ
La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, wa huwa ‘ala kulli shay’in qadir. La ilaha illallahu wahdah, anjaza wa’dah, wa nasara ‘abdah, wa hazamal-ahzaba wahdah.
There is no god but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He is over all things capable. He fulfilled His promise, helped His servant, and defeated the confederates alone.
Say the dhikr, then make your own dua. Repeat this three times.
05 · COMPLETING UMRAH
Finish your Umrah
After Sa'i
Complete Umrah by shaving or shortening the hair. There is no particular authentic dua that must be recited at this point.
رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا
Rabbana taqabbal minna.
Our Lord, accept from us.
Remember: You can make sincere personal dua in your own words throughout Umrah.

Personal reference only. For religious practice, follow your qualified scholar/guide and current official guidance.
