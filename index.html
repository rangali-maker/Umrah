Umrah Duas

A simple pocket guide — just the essentials.

Intention
Entering Umrah
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ عُمْرَةً
Labbayka Allahumma ‘Umrah.
Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah.
The intention is in the heart. When entering the rite, say the Talbiyah/intention phrase above.
Saudi Ministry of Hajj & Umrah Umrah Guide.
Masjid al-Haram
Entering the Masjid
اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Allahumma iftah li abwaba rahmatik.
O Allah, open for me the gates of Your mercy.
Leaving the Masjid
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
Allahumma inni as’aluka min fadlik.
O Allah, I ask You from Your bounty.
Tawaf
After Tawaf — at Maqam Ibrahim
وَاتَّخِذُوا مِنْ مَقَامِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ مُصَلًّى
Wattakhidhu min maqami Ibrahima musalla.
And take the standing-place of Abraham as a place of prayer.
After completing Tawaf, it is recommended to pray two rak'ahs, with Maqam Ibrahim between you and the Ka'bah when practical. This Qur'anic verse is associated with that prayer.
At the Black Stone
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allahu akbar.
Allah is the Greatest.
Between the Yemeni Corner & Black Stone
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina ‘adhaban-nar.
Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
Important: There is no authentic specific dua for each of the seven circuits. During the rest of Tawaf, make any sincere dua, dhikr, or recite Qur'an.
Based on the Sunnah guidance cited by Islam Q&A; see also the Saudi Ministry guide.
Safa & Marwa
Upon approaching Safa — first time
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ فَمَنْ حَجَّ الْبَيْتَ أَوِ اعْتَمَرَ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيْهِ أَنْ يَطَّوَّفَ بِهِمَا وَمَنْ تَطَوَّعَ خَيْرًا فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ شَاكِرٌ عَلِيمٌ
Innas-Safa wal-Marwata min sha'a'irillah. Faman hajj-al-bayta awi'tamara fala junaha 'alayhi an yattawwafa bihima. Wa man tatawwa'a khayran fa-innallaha shakirun 'alim.
Indeed, Safa and Marwa are among the symbols of Allah. So whoever performs Hajj or Umrah, there is no blame upon him for walking between them. And whoever does good voluntarily — then indeed Allah is Appreciative, All-Knowing.
Then say: Abda’u bima bada’allahu bih — “I begin with what Allah began with.” The verse is recited when first approaching Safa.
At Safa & Marwa
لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ
La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, wa huwa ‘ala kulli shay’in qadir. La ilaha illallahu wahdah, anjaza wa’dah, wa nasara ‘abdah, wa hazamal-ahzaba wahdah.

There is no god but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He is over all things capable. There is no god but Allah alone; He fulfilled His promise, helped His servant, and defeated the confederates alone.
At Safa and Marwa, say this dhikr, then make your own dua. Repeat the dhikr and dua three times. The verse above is for the first approach to Safa; at Marwa, perform the same dhikr and make your own dua.
General Duas
Duas for everyday life, protection, forgiveness and all your needs
Pain, fear or discomfort
أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ وَقُدْرَتِهِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا أَجِدُ وَأُحَاذِرُ
A‘udhu billahi wa qudratihi min sharri ma ajidu wa uhadir.
I seek refuge in Allah and in His Power from the evil of what I find and what I fear.
Sahih Muslim 2202
Forgiveness for major and minor sins
اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذُنُوبِيَ الْكَبِيرَةَ وَالصَّغِيرَةَ، الْمَعْلُومَةَ وَالْمَخْفِيَّةَ، وَطَهِّرْ قَلْبِي مِنَ الْخَطَايَا
Allahumma aghfir li dhunubi al-kabirata was-saghirata, al-ma‘lumata wal-makhfiyyata, wa tahhir qalbi minal-khataya.
O Allah, forgive my major and minor sins, those known and hidden, and purify my heart from wrongdoing.
Reference shown on your image: Abu Dawud / Tirmidhi
For an easy and good marriage
اللَّهُمَّ يَسِّرْ لِي زَوَاجِي، وَاجْعَلْهُ خَيْرًا لِي فِي دِينِي وَدُنْيَايَ
Allahumma yassir li zawaji, waj‘alhu khayran li fi dini wa dunyaya.
O Allah, make my marriage easy for me, and make it good for me in my religion and worldly life.

For healing
اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ النَّاسِ، أَذْهِبِ الْبَأْسَ، وَاشْفِ أَنْتَ الشَّافِي، لَا شِفَاءَ إِلَّا شِفَاؤُكَ، شِفَاءً لَا يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا
Allahumma Rabban-nas, adhhib il-ba’s, washfi antash-Shafi, la shifa’a illa shifa’uk, shifa’an la yughadiru saqaman.
O Allah, Lord of mankind, remove the suffering and heal; You are the Healer. There is no cure except Your cure, a cure that leaves no sickness behind.
Sahih al-Bukhari 5743; Sahih Muslim 2191
For relief from overthinking and worry
اللَّهُمَّ أَذْهِبْ عَنِّي هَمَّ الْفِكْرِ وَالْهَمَّ، وَأَعِنِّي عَلَى تَدْبِيرِ أُمُورِي خَيْرًا مِمَّا أُدَبِّرُهُ
Allahumma adhhib ‘anni hammal-fikri wal-hamm, wa a‘inni ‘ala tadbiri umuri khayran mimma udabbiruhu.
O Allah, relieve me from the burden of overthinking and worries, and help me manage my affairs better than I could myself.

For good in this world and the Hereafter
اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا وَآتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Allahummaghfir lana warhamna, wa atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina ‘adhaban-nar.
O Allah, forgive us, have mercy on us, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and save us from the punishment of the Fire.
Image cites Anas ibn Malik; wording includes Qur’an 2:201
For the continuance of blessings
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ زَوَالِ نِعْمَتِكَ، وَتَحَوُّلِ عَافِيَتِكَ، وَفُجَاءَةِ نِقْمَتِكَ، وَجَمِيعِ سَخَطِكَ
Allahumma inni a‘udhu bika min zawali ni‘matika, wa tahawwuli ‘afiyatika, wa fuja’ati niqmatika, wa jami‘i sakhatika.
O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the disappearance of Your blessing, the loss of good health, the suddenness of Your punishment, and all that displeases You.

Protection and ruqyah
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ، مِنْ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ يُؤْذِيكَ، مِنْ شَرِّ كُلِّ نَفْسٍ أَوْ عَيْنٍ أَوْ حَاسِدٍ، اللَّهُ يَشْفِيكَ، بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ
Bismillahi arqika, min kulli shay’in yu’dhika, min sharri kulli nafsin aw ‘aynin aw hasidin, Allahu yashfika, bismillahi arqika.
In the Name of Allah I perform ruqyah for you, from everything that harms you, from the evil of every soul or envious eye. May Allah heal you. In the Name of Allah I perform ruqyah for you.

For forgiveness and repentance

Ask Allah with His Greatest Names — forgiveness
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ يَا اللَّهُ، الْأَحَدُ الصَّمَدُ، الَّذِي لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ، أَنْ تَغْفِرَ لِي ذُنُوبِي، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْغَفُورُ الرَّحِيمُ

Ask Allah with His Greatest Names — praise and majesty
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ بِأَنَّ لَكَ الْحَمْدَ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، الْمَنَّانُ، بَدِيعُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ، يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ، إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ

O Allah, I ask You because all praise belongs to You; there is none worthy of worship except You, the Bestower, Creator of the heavens and the earth, Possessor of majesty and honor, Ever-Living, Sustainer. I ask You.
